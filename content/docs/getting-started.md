# Getting started

This page walks you through your first download. You don't need to know anything about yt-dlp or FFmpeg.

## 1. Check the helper tools

Open waydlp. The bottom of the sidebar shows:

- **• yt-dlp ready** — good. If it says _missing_, open **Settings → yt-dlp & FFmpeg** and press **Install yt-dlp**.
- **• FFmpeg ready** — good. If it says _missing_, press **Install FFmpeg** (Windows) or install it with your package manager (Linux). Without FFmpeg you can still download using the _Original file_ mode.

## 2. Paste a link

Copy the address of a video or song from your browser. In waydlp, press **📋 Paste** on the Home page — or just press **Ctrl+V** anywhere outside a text field.

## 3. Analyze

Press **Analyze**. waydlp asks yt-dlp what the page offers and shows the real title, creator, duration, thumbnail and the qualities that exist. Nothing is downloaded yet.

## 4. Choose what you want

- **Video** — pick a quality (only the real ones are listed) and a container. _MP4_ plays almost everywhere.
- **Audio** — pick a format such as _MP3_. The bitrate is an _encoder target_: converting can't add quality that wasn't in the source.
- **Video only** — no sound.
- **Original file** — exactly what the site provides; no conversion and no FFmpeg needed.

Or choose a **preset** such as _MP4 1080p_ or _MP3_.

## 5. Download

Press **⬇ Download**. The **Queue** shows real progress: size, speed and time left. When it's done, press **▶ Open** or **📂 Show in folder**. Finished downloads are also listed in **History**.

## The one-click way

On Home, **⬇ Quick download** skips the analysis step and uses your _default preset_ (change it in Settings → Downloads). Playlist links are always previewed first, so you never download hundreds of files by accident.
