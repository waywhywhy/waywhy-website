# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 4 November 2026_

waydlp is built to respect your privacy. This policy covers the waydlp apps (desktop and Android) and this website.

## The apps

- **No accounts.** You never sign up or log in to waydlp.
- **No telemetry, analytics, crash reporting or advertising identifiers.**
- **Your data stays on your device.** Settings, download history and downloaded files are stored locally. You can turn history off, limit how long it is kept, or clear it.
- **Network connections happen only when needed:**
  - to the websites you ask waydlp to download from (performed by yt-dlp), and to load their thumbnails (can be turned off);
  - to GitHub, when you install or update yt-dlp/FFmpeg, or check for a new waydlp version. Automatic update checks are **off** by default.
- **Clipboard:** waydlp reads the clipboard when you press Paste or Ctrl+V. Optional clipboard watching is **off** by default and, when enabled, only runs while waydlp is focused and only suggests a link.
- **Cookies:** waydlp never reads your browsers' cookies. If you choose a cookies file for a site that requires sign-in, it is used only locally by yt-dlp and is never uploaded anywhere; cookie headers are removed from logs.
- **Logs** are stored locally and are only shared if you choose to attach them to a bug report.

Websites you download from have their own privacy policies; your requests to them are made from your device, like visiting them in a browser.

## This website

- The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes technical request data (such as IP addresses) to deliver pages and protect against abuse — see [Vercel's privacy policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).
- We use **no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts**.
- Release information is loaded from GitHub on our server, so your browser does not contact the GitHub API. Download links point to GitHub Releases; downloading a file connects you to GitHub, under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- The download page detects your operating system in your browser to pre-select a tab; nothing is sent anywhere.

## Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the website's repository history.
